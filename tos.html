Terms of Service

Last updated: October 31, 2026

VoidGifts is a Telegram mini app where you open daily gifts, spin prize wheels, play the Rocket and Gift Battle games and collect virtual items. By using it you agree to these terms.

1. Who can use VoidGifts

You need a Telegram account and must be at least 18 years old, or the age of digital consent where you live if that is higher. If you use VoidGifts, you confirm that you meet this requirement.

2. Coins, Stars and gifts

  • Coins and the in-app Stars balance are virtual items used inside VoidGifts. Coins have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for money. Stars are bought with real money (Telegram Stars or TON), but once added they can only be spent inside VoidGifts and cannot be withdrawn, transferred or refunded.
  • Prizes are drawn at random by the prize wheels. The Daily wheel is free. It can be used once every 24 hours. The Void wheel costs 50 Stars per spin; other paid wheels show their price in the app.
  • Gifts and NFT gifts you win are added to your inventory. Some can be claimed to your Telegram account. NFT gifts are delivered after a waiting period, which is shown as a countdown in your inventory. Claiming an NFT gift requires a recent Stars top-up of at least 25 Stars, which is used up by that claim.
  • You can convert a prize to coins instead of claiming it, using the value shown on the prize.

3. Purchases

You can add Stars to your balance by paying with Telegram Stars or with TON. Payments are processed by Telegram or by the TON network and your wallet provider, not by VoidGifts. Transactions on the TON network are final once confirmed. All purchases are final and we do not offer refunds. If a payment went through but your balance was not credited, contact @voidgiftssupportbot and we will fix it.

4. Channel subscription

To spin the wheels you must be subscribed to our Telegram channel. We check your membership on our server. If you leave the channel, spinning is paused until you rejoin.

5. Promo codes

Promo codes are single-use per account, may expire, and may be withdrawn. Some promo codes are only available to accounts that have topped up with at least 2 TON in total. Codes shared in bulk or obtained by abuse may be voided.

6. Rocket

  • In Rocket you place a bet before each round with Stars or with a gift from your inventory. A rocket then climbs and a multiplier grows until it crashes at a point the server decides.
  • If you cash out before the crash, you receive your bet times the multiplier at that moment. A gift is upgraded to the best item worth up to its value times the multiplier; any remainder is paid in coins (gifts) or Stars (NFTs). You can also set an automatic cash-out.
  • If the rocket crashes before you cash out, your bet is lost. Cash-outs are processed on our server, so a slow connection can make you miss the moment you meant to cash out. We are not responsible for that, and results cannot be reversed.
  • The game has a built-in house edge, so over time the house is expected to win more than it pays out. Do not bet more than you can afford to lose.

7. Gift Battle

  • In Gift Battle you stake gifts from your inventory (up to the per-round limit shown in the app). All players in a round put their gifts into one pot. Your chance of winning is the value of your gifts divided by the value of everything in the pot. One winner is then drawn by the server.
  • Gift values. Everyday gifts count at their Telegram Star price. NFT gifts count at their current market price, taken from the marketplace price list we use and refreshed regularly. The value is fixed at the moment you place your stake. Because market prices change, the value of an NFT can differ from the price in your inventory or in other parts of the app.
  • Winner and fee. The winner receives the gifts in the pot, minus a house fee. The fee depends on the winner's chance: there is no fee when the winner held 90% or more of the pot, and the fee grows as the winner's chance gets smaller, up to 10% of the pot's value. The fee is taken as whole gifts from the other players' stakes. It never exceeds the fee amount and never comes from the winner's own gifts. The current fee for your stake is shown before you join and may be changed with notice in the app.
  • Once you have staked a gift you cannot take it back. If you are the only player in a round, your gifts are returned. If a round fails for a technical reason, all staked gifts are returned.
  • Gift Battle is a game of chance and you can lose the gifts you stake. Make sure that playing games of chance is allowed where you live, and play responsibly.

8. Fairness of rounds

Rocket and Gift Battle results are decided by our server using a random secret chosen before betting opens. A fingerprint (hash) of that secret is fixed first, and the secret itself is published when the round ends, so a finished round can be checked against its fingerprint. If you want help checking a round, contact support. Results of finished rounds are final.

Recent prizes may also be shown to all players in the Live gifts strip on the home screen. It shows only the name of the gift and when it was won, not who won it.
