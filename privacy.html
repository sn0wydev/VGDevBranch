This page explains what VoidGifts collects when you use it inside Telegram, why, and what you can control.

What we collect

  • From your Telegram profile: your Telegram user ID, username, first and last name, and profile photo link.
  • Your game data: your coin and Stars balances, your prizes and their status, and your settings, including whether you appear on the leaderboard.
  • Payments: a reference for each Stars or TON purchase so we can credit your balance. If you connect a TON wallet, your wallet address is used to request payment. We never see your private keys.
  • Channel membership: whether you are subscribed to our channel, checked with Telegram when you try to spin.

What is stored on your device

The app keeps your settings, redeemed promo codes and TON wallet connection details in Telegram cloud storage or your browser's local storage, so they persist between visits.

Why we use it

  • To run the game: balances, prizes, spins, claims and purchases.
  • To show the leaderboard, if you have chosen to appear on it.
  • To prevent abuse such as duplicate accounts and bot activity.
