Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This page explains what VoidGifts collects when you use it inside Telegram, why, and what you can control.

What we collect

  • From your Telegram profile: your Telegram user ID, username, first and last name, and profile photo link.
  • Your game data: your coin and Stars balances, your prizes and their status, and your settings, including whether you appear on the leaderboard.
  • Payments: a reference for each Stars or TON purchase so we can credit your balance. If you connect a TON wallet, your wallet address is used to request payment. We never see your private keys.
  • Rocket and Gift Battle: your bets and stakes, the gifts you put in, the result of each round and any gifts or Stars you win or lose, plus the fingerprint and secret of each round so results can be verified.
  • Channel membership: whether you are subscribed to our channel, checked with Telegram when you try to spin.

What other players can see

  • In Rocket and Gift Battle rounds: other players can see your display name (your Telegram username, or your first name if you have no username), your avatar, what you bet or staked, your chance and the result. Recent winners of Gift Battle are listed with their name and the pot value.
  • If you turn off Show in Leaderboard, you appear as "Player" without an avatar in these places too.
  • Live gifts: the home screen shows recent prizes won by players. It shows only the gift name and time, never a name, ID or avatar.

What is stored on your device

The app keeps your settings, redeemed promo codes and TON wallet connection details in Telegram cloud storage or your browser's local storage, so they persist between visits.

Why we use it

  • To run the game: balances, prizes, spins, claims and purchases.
  • To show the leaderboard and the players in Rocket and Gift Battle rounds, if you have chosen to appear.
  • To run and verify game rounds and to settle disputes about them.
  • To prevent abuse such as duplicate accounts and bot activity.

Who else handles it

Telegram (the platform and Stars payments), the TON network and your wallet provider (TON payments), and our hosting and database providers, Railway (servers and databases) and GitHub Pages (the app's web files). The app also loads some code libraries from public content delivery networks (cdnjs and unpkg), which can see your IP address when they serve those files. We do not use analytics or advertising providers and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Your choices

  • Turn off Show in Leaderboard under Settings, Privacy, to hide your name and avatar from other players.
  • Reset All Data under Settings clears the data stored on your device.
  • To ask us to delete or export the data we hold about you, contact @voidgiftssupportbot.

Retention

We keep your account data (profile details, balances, prizes) for as long as your account is active, and delete or anonymize it on request. Records of purchases and prize claims are kept for as long as needed to credit your balance, resolve disputes and prevent abuse.

Children

VoidGifts is not intended for anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used it, contact us and we will remove the data.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions: @voidgiftssupportbot.
